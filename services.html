SKYLINE / SERVICE DIRECTORY

Construction support, explained clearly.

A practical guide to the types of construction, civil-work, procurement and coordination requirements you can discuss with SKYLINE. Every project is scoped individually before any quotation or commitment.

DISCUSS YOUR PROJECT ↗

Start with the scope, not assumptions.

Construction cost and delivery depend on drawings, site conditions, quantities, material specifications, access, programme and approval requirements. Share what is known; unclear items can be listed for confirmation before work is priced.

New constructionCivil worksProcurementFinishingTrading assistanceHow it works
01 / BUILDING WORK

New building construction

Discuss residential, commercial or other building requirements from early scope definition through the sequence of construction activities.

  • Plot location, dimensions, access and site constraints
  • Architectural drawings, structural drawings and approved plans
  • Covered area, floors, basement and intended use
  • Grey-structure versus complete-build scope
  • Finish level, fixtures, MEP requirements and target programme

Structural design, engineering approvals and specialist sign-offs must be provided by appropriately qualified professionals.

02 / SITE & CIVIL

Civil works and site development

Define the exact work package and measurable quantities before comparing quotations.

  • Site clearing, excavation and earthwork
  • Filling, compaction and level preparation
  • Foundations and reinforced-concrete elements to approved design
  • Boundary walls, paving, drainage and external works
  • Concrete works, masonry, plaster and repairs

Soil conditions, utility locations, dewatering, disposal and access can materially change scope and cost.

03 / PROCUREMENT

Building material sourcing

Material enquiries are easier to price accurately when they include a BOQ or a clear specification.

  • Steel / saria: grade, diameter, quantity and approved manufacturer
  • Cement: type, brand preference, bags and delivery point
  • Bricks / blocks: class, size, strength and quantity
  • Sand / crush: grading, size, source and intended use
  • MEP and finishes: brand, model, rating and datasheet

Ask for itemised written quotations that identify unit, quantity, tax, freight and validity period.

04 / FINISHING & MEP

Interior, exterior and services

Finishes should be selected as coordinated systems, not as isolated product names.

  • Waterproofing systems and substrate preparation
  • Tiles, stone, adhesives, grout and movement joints
  • Doors, windows, glazing, hardware and sealants
  • Paint systems, primers and surface preparation
  • Plumbing, electrical, lighting and sanitary fittings
  • Ceilings, partitions, insulation and facade interfaces
05 / TRADING & CONNECT

Sales and purchase assistance

Share the nature of the buying or selling requirement and the information available for an initial discussion.

  • Property type and exact location
  • Plot/building size and relevant documentation
  • Asking or target price and transaction timeline
  • Any authority, title, possession or approval questions to verify

Do not treat marketing material as proof of title, NOC or legal status. Verify records with the relevant authority and qualified legal adviser.

06 / PROJECT COORDINATION

Planning, sequencing and communication

Good coordination depends on an agreed scope, responsibilities and a record of decisions.

  • Break work into measurable packages and milestones
  • Identify long-lead materials and delivery dependencies
  • Record drawings, revisions, approvals and site instructions
  • Track changes against the original scope and budget
  • Agree inspection and handover documentation
A SIMPLE STARTING PROCESS

From first message to a clear scope.

1. Share the basics

Send site location, work type, approximate dimensions, project stage and your expected timeline.

2. Provide documents

Where available, attach drawings, BOQ, photos, material schedule, specifications and authority correspondence.

3. Clarify assumptions

List inclusions, exclusions, quantities, brands, quality grades, delivery terms and any unknown site conditions.

4. Review a written quotation

Compare scope, unit rates, taxes, transport, payment milestones, validity and expected programme before acceptance.
