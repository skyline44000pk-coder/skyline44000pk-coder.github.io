Reinforcing Steel (Saria) Inspection & Storage Checklist

Receiving, storage and pre-pour checks for residential construction sites in Islamabad and Rawalpindi.

Purpose: Use this field checklist to organize observations and records. It does not set design values or decide structural acceptance. Approved drawings, the bar-bending schedule, specifications, applicable standards and the responsible engineer govern.

Important: Do not decide grade, lap length, anchorage, cover, bar spacing, welding, bending or acceptance by guesswork. Obtain the project's written criteria. A thin, adherent surface rust film is not the same as loose, flaky corrosion; pitting, section loss or uncertain condition must be referred to the engineer before use.

Reference & caution

  • Project approved drawings, specifications and bar-bending schedules take precedence.
  • Seek written engineering disposition for corrosion, section loss, nonconforming bars, unapproved welding, bending or changes in placement.
  • General specification guidance supports storing reinforcement above ground and avoiding contamination or damage; acceptance criteria must be project-specific.

Reference: AJK Government General Specification (Building) and ACI education bulletin on reinforcement. These sources are guidance examples; follow the specifications applicable to your project.
