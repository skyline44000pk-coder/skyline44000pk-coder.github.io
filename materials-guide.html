SKYLINE / MATERIAL KNOWLEDGE CENTRE

Know what you are buying.

A detailed, practical checklist for specifying, inspecting and ordering common building materials in Pakistan. Always follow the approved drawings, project specification and advice of the relevant engineer or designer.

REQUEST MATERIAL QUOTATION ↗

Specification first. Price second.

Two products with similar names may differ in grade, size, performance, brand, batch or suitability. A useful quotation should state the exact item, unit, quantity, delivery location and commercial terms. Never substitute a structural or safety-critical product without the designer's written approval.

01 / REINFORCEMENT

Steel / Saria

Reinforcement bars are selected to match the structural drawings, bar bending schedule and engineer's specification.

  • Confirm grade and applicable product standard on the purchase order.
  • Check nominal diameter, bar count, length and declared weight.
  • Inspect manufacturer marks and traceability on delivered bars.
  • Request mill test certificates where specified; reconcile batch details.
  • Look for serious corrosion, pitting, bends or physical damage.
  • Store off the ground and separate sizes/grades to avoid mix-ups.

Do not estimate structural steel by appearance or replace the specified grade/diameter based only on price.

02 / BINDERS

Cement

Cement type and performance requirements depend on the application, exposure conditions and approved mix/design.

  • Confirm cement type, manufacturer, bag weight and project specification.
  • Inspect intact bags, batch information and manufacturing date.
  • Reject hardened, damp or damaged bags.
  • Store on a raised dry platform, protected from rain and ground moisture.
  • Keep stacks manageable and use older stock first.
  • Do not mix different cement types without technical approval.

For concrete, follow the approved mix design and quality-control plan rather than a generic site ratio.

03 / WALLING

Bricks and concrete blocks

Masonry units should be suitable for their intended structural or partition use and consistent with the drawings.

  • Agree the class, dimensions, tolerances and quantity before ordering.
  • Inspect representative samples for shape, cracks and consistency.
  • Check block strength, density, curing and intended application.
  • Clarify loading, unloading, breakage allowance and return terms.
  • Confirm mortar and joint requirements with the project specification.

Do not assume a wall unit is load-bearing simply because it is solid or thick; follow the structural design.

04 / CONCRETE INGREDIENTS

Sand and crush / aggregates

Aggregate grading, cleanliness and source influence concrete and mortar performance.

  • State intended use: concrete, masonry mortar, plaster or bedding.
  • Confirm grading and nominal maximum aggregate size.
  • Check for excessive silt, clay, organic matter or contamination.
  • Agree source, measured unit, moisture condition and delivery quantity.
  • Use the mix design's specified aggregate proportions and quality tests.

Clarify whether the quoted quantity is loose volume, truck capacity or weighed quantity.

05 / BUILDING SERVICES

Plumbing and electrical

MEP products must match the design loads, pressure, temperature, environment and applicable safety requirements.

  • Pipes: material, diameter, pressure class, joining system and intended service.
  • Fittings/valves: compatibility, rating, size and access for maintenance.
  • Cables: conductor material, cross-section, insulation, voltage and current rating.
  • Switchgear: breaking capacity, poles, ratings and certification where specified.
  • Conduits/accessories: size, material, fire/sunlight exposure and installation method.
  • Ask for datasheets and genuine product identification.

Electrical sizing, protection and plumbing layouts should be confirmed by qualified professionals.

06 / PROTECTION

Waterproofing materials

Choose a system based on the location, water exposure, substrate, movement and finish above it.

  • Identify roof, bathroom, tank, basement or retaining-wall application.
  • Check substrate preparation, primer and moisture requirements.
  • Confirm number of coats, membrane thickness and coverage rate.
  • Detail corners, pipe penetrations, joints, outlets and upstands.
  • Follow curing times and flood-test requirements where applicable.
  • Keep product batch, expiry and installation records.
07 / FINISHING

Tiles, stone, paint and coatings

Appearance, durability and installation performance depend on the complete system.

  • Tiles: size, shade/calibre batch, finish, slip performance and wastage.
  • Stone: type, thickness, porosity, sealing and fixing method.
  • Adhesives/grout: substrate, tile type, joint width and wet-area suitability.
  • Paint: surface preparation, primer, coating system and coverage.
  • Compare sample boards under the actual lighting before bulk purchase.
08 / OPENINGS & INTERIORS

Doors, windows, glass, ceilings and insulation

Coordinate dimensions and interfaces before fabrication or installation.

  • Windows: profile series, glazing, hardware, drainage and air/water sealing.
  • Glass: type, thickness, safety treatment and support details as designed.
  • Doors: core, frame, finish, fire rating where required and hardware.
  • Ceilings/partitions: board type, thickness, framing and access panels.
  • Insulation: thermal/acoustic target, thickness, vapour control and fire performance.
  • Facade: substrate, anchors, movement joints and weatherproofing details.
BEFORE YOU PLACE AN ORDER

A complete material enquiry checklist.

Technical details

  • Exact product name and intended application
  • Brand / manufacturer and approved alternatives
  • Grade, size, standard, model or datasheet
  • Quantity and unit of measurement
  • Sample / inspection / test requirements

Commercial details

  • Delivery address and required date
  • Unit rate and total amount
  • Tax status and freight / unloading
  • Payment terms and quotation validity
  • Shortage, damage and return policy

Need help preparing a material list?

Send the BOQ, drawing or item list. Include quantities and the delivery location so the enquiry can be reviewed properly.
