CDA and NUST reported collaboration on smog and environmental pollution

News reports dated 28 November 2026 say CDA and NUST agreed to work together on smog mitigation, environmental protection, research and development, and pollution monitoring in Islamabad. The reported plan includes a dedicated fund and a committee expected to provide fortnightly progress reports.

  • Joint research and environmental-protection initiatives.
  • Reported dedicated fund for smog mitigation, environment and research projects.
  • Road and kerbstone design review to improve drainage and reduce dust/debris accumulation.
  • Reported monitoring of pollution sources and alerts, alongside dust-control measures.

Status: This is a news-reported initiative summary, not a published construction regulation, road approval, tender or verified implementation schedule. Follow CDA for official documents and subsequent updates.

Sources: Dawn, 28 November 2026 · Aaj English, 27 November 2026
