CDA BOARD MONITOR · 27 NOVEMBER 2026

22nd CDA Board Meeting: Islamabad Avenue & E-12/4

Meeting held on 25 November 2026 · Release published 26 November 2026

Source-based summary: The 22nd CDA Board meeting considered multiple agenda items and approved the following reported decisions. This page summarizes the authority’s release; it is not a legal notice or implementation confirmation.

  • Islamabad Avenue, Zone IV: A committee was approved to formulate bylaws for the under-construction avenue.
  • Sector E-12/4: Board approved early completion of development work and development works in several pockets, including the main road and service road.
  • Residential-sector plot subdivisions: Pending subdivision cases are to be resolved under the existing policy; the board directed a review of the subdivision fee and recommendations to be presented.
  • Capital Hospital: Establishment of the CDA Health Foundation was approved, along with formal launch of institution-based private practice (IBPP), as described in the release.
Investor and owner caution: A board approval or committee formation is not itself a final bylaw, project completion certificate, plot approval or property-specific permission. Check subsequent official notifications and the relevant CDA file before relying on a decision.
