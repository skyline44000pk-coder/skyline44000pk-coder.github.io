Official verification links

Check the regulator first.

For Islamabad and Rawalpindi, use the official authority records. Read the exact phase/block and status, not just the society name.

  • CDA approved / status list
  • CDA illegal schemes list
  • RDA approved housing societies
  • RDA illegal housing societies
  • Punjab PHATA
  • PEC building codes & standards
An approved Layout Plan (LOP) is not automatically the same as an issued NOC. An authority list entry does not certify an individual plot, title or seller. Always confirm directly with the regulator and a qualified lawyer.
