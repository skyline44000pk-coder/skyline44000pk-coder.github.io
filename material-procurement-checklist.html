Material Procurement & Delivery Checklist

Use this practical checklist before requesting quotes, confirming an order or accepting a delivery.

Price comparisons only work when suppliers are quoting the same specification, unit and delivery conditions. This checklist is a planning aid—not an engineering specification. Follow the approved drawings, contract and instructions of the project engineer.

2 · Material-specific checks

Steel / saria

Confirm grade, diameter, length, mill/brand and quantity by weight. Check markings, rust/contamination and certificates or test reports where required. Engineer approval governs.

Cement

Confirm brand, type, bag weight, batch/date, packaging and storage. Avoid damp, hardened or damaged bags; rotate stock and store off the ground under cover.

Bricks / blocks

Agree type, dimensions, class and count/measurement basis. Inspect sample consistency, breakage, size variation and delivery losses.

Sand & aggregate

Confirm source/type, unit (CFT, trolley or truck), measured capacity, cleanliness, grading and delivery basis. Do not compare different truck capacities as equivalent.

Electrical & plumbing

Specify approved brand, product standard, rating, size, pressure/class, model and accessories. Keep packaging, labels and warranties.

Tiles & finishes

Confirm size, shade, calibre, batch, surface/finish, quantity and extra stock for cuts. Review a sample before purchase.
