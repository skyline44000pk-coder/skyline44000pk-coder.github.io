Material supply

The essentials, sourced with care.

Tell us your required quantity, quality and delivery location. Our team can help coordinate suitable options.

Steel / SariaReinforcement bars sourced against the structural drawings and BOQ. Confirm manufacturer, grade, nominal diameter, weight per bundle, mill markings and test documentation before acceptance.
CementOptions for general construction and specified concrete or masonry applications. Check brand, cement type, batch details, manufacturing date, intact bags and dry storage; use only the engineer-approved specification.
BricksBrick options for load-bearing or partition masonry as specified. Assess dimensions, shape, firing consistency, cracks, soundness and sample quality; agree class, quantity and breakage terms before delivery.
Concrete BlocksHollow or solid block options for suitable wall applications. Confirm dimensions, density/strength requirement, curing, visible cracks and intended use with the project designer.
SandFine aggregate options for mortar, plaster and concrete where suitable. Check cleanliness, grading and excessive silt or organic contamination against the approved mix and specification.
Crush / AggregatesCoarse aggregate options in specified sizes for concrete and other works. Confirm grading, cleanliness, source and suitability for the mix design before ordering bulk quantities.
Plumbing & ElectricalDiscuss pipes, fittings, conduits, cables and accessories by approved brand, size, rating and project specification. Product datasheets and compatibility matter more than price alone.
Waterproofing & FinishesSource membranes, coatings, tile adhesives and finishing materials according to location, substrate and manufacturer instructions. Confirm coverage, shelf life and application requirements.

SKYLINE can coordinate quotations for Islamabad and Rawalpindi projects. Share the BOQ or item list, required brand/grade, quantity, delivery address and preferred delivery date. Final brand, specification, availability, taxes, freight and rates are confirmed in the written quotation; no unverified stock or fixed market price is promised.
