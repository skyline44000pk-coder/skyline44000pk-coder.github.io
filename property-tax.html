Annual property tax · authority assessment required

Annual property tax is not one uniform rate for Islamabad and Rawalpindi. It depends on jurisdiction, valuation/rentable value, use, covered area, exemptions and current notified rules.

Annual tax payable
Rate requires official confirmation

No amount is shown until the competent authority's current valuation table, tax schedule and applicable exemptions are verified for this exact property.

For Rawalpindi, confirm whether the property falls under Punjab Excise & Taxation, a municipal/local authority or a cantonment board. For Islamabad, confirm ICT/CDA jurisdiction and the applicable property-tax schedule. A housing-society location alone does not establish the tax authority.
