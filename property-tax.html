PROPERTY TAX & TRANSFER DESK

Understand the charges before a property transaction

Buyer-side items to verify

  • Applicable federal advance tax and buyer filer/non-filer status under current law
  • Provincial/territorial stamp duty and registration charges
  • Capital value tax or other locally applicable levies, if any
  • Authority/society transfer, membership, NDC and documentation fees
  • Outstanding property tax, utility bills and service charges allocated by agreement

Seller-side items to verify

  • Federal advance tax on sale and any applicable withholding
  • Capital gains tax position, acquisition cost, holding period and exemptions
  • Outstanding annual property tax and authority dues
  • Brokerage/commission and legal/documentation expenses
  • Contractual allocation of transfer and clearance costs

Before generating a challan

  • Confirm transaction date and the law/rates effective on that date
  • Check valuation method and property category used by the authority
  • Confirm taxpayer status on the official FBR system
  • Use the correct authority, property identifier and payment head
  • Retain challan, bank receipt, return/withholding certificate and transfer record

Why estimates can differ

Tax outcomes depend on location, property type, declared/documented value, valuation tables, filer status, acquisition and sale dates, exemptions, ownership structure and current amendments. A calculator cannot determine every legal fact or replace an official assessment.

Use the official FBR portal and the relevant provincial/ICT authority for current rules. Obtain a written calculation from a qualified tax practitioner before signing or paying. Never pay a personal account based only on a forwarded challan image.
