Practical site guidance

Before you buy materials or start building

Short checks that help reduce avoidable delays, disputes and rework.

11 / PROJECT PLANNING

Plan a construction project in Islamabad or Rawalpindi

Use our detailed planning checklist to coordinate plot and approval checks, drawings, budget categories, material procurement, site quality, safety, payment milestones and project records before work begins.

Read the Twin Cities construction planning guide ↗

01 / APPROVALS

Verify the plot and scheme first

Match the exact scheme, block, plot number and phase against the relevant authority's current records. Ask for the approved layout plan and current NOC/status; do not rely only on a marketing brochure or a seller's screenshot.

02 / STRUCTURE

Check steel before unloading

Confirm mill/brand marks, grade, nominal diameter and bar count against the structural engineer's schedule and purchase order. Record the delivered weight and reject bars with serious pitting, damage or mismatched specification.

03 / CEMENT

Store cement dry and off the floor

Check the manufacturing date, intact bags and batch information. Keep bags on a raised dry platform, away from walls and rain; use older stock first and do not use hardened or moisture-damaged bags.

04 / BRICKWORK

Agree brick quality and delivery terms

Inspect a sample for consistent dimensions, shape and visible cracks. Put the brick class, quantity, loading, breakage allowance and delivered-to-site price in writing before placing a bulk order.

05 / CONCRETE

Do not guess the concrete mix

Use the mix design and strength specified by the structural engineer. Confirm aggregate grading, water quality, batching, compaction and curing on site; a generic cement-to-sand ratio is not a substitute for a designed structural mix.

06 / BUDGET

Build a BOQ before fixing the budget

Separate structure, masonry, plaster, waterproofing, electrical, plumbing, doors/windows, finishes, external works and professional/approval costs. Keep a contingency and update rates using written local quotes.

07 / PRICE DATA

Read the correct PBS price series

The weekly Sensitive Price Indicator (SPI) tracks 51 essential household items from 50 markets in 17 cities. It includes selected construction-related items such as cement and some labour observations, but it is not a complete construction-material schedule. For iron bars, bricks, sand and bajri, use the separate monthly Urban Construction Items Prices workbook and check the exact item, city, unit and reference month. If a material or specification is absent, leave it as quote-on-request rather than borrowing a different item or unit.

11 / CURING

Concrete curing: keep moisture and protection consistent

Follow the approved curing period and method for the concrete mix and site conditions. A common reference is at least 7 days for ordinary structural concrete, but temperature, cementitious materials, testing and project specifications can require longer or different procedures. Avoid drying-and-wetting cycles, protect the surface and record curing checks. Open the practical curing guide ↗

09 / CONCRETE POUR

Pre-pour slab inspection checklist

Before a slab pour, have the responsible engineer or site supervisor check the approved drawings and the work on site. Confirm these items and record any corrections before concrete arrives:

  • Rebar size, spacing, laps, anchorage and cover against the structural drawings; verify chairs/spacers and that bars are not displaced.
  • Beam, slab, column and opening dimensions; levels, slopes and embedded sleeves/conduits as coordinated with services.
  • Formwork/shuttering line, level, joints, supports, bracing, cleanliness and stability; no visible movement or leakage risk.
  • Concrete mix/specification, delivery tickets, planned sampling/testing, placement sequence, vibration and curing arrangements.
  • Access, safe work platforms, pump/vehicle positioning, weather and pour-day communication.
  • Resolve and document deviations; do not proceed on a structural change without the responsible engineer’s approval.

This checklist is a site aid, not a substitute for project-specific inspection, approved drawings or the applicable code. Refer to the Pakistan Engineering Council’s Building Code of Pakistan 2021, including structural design, special inspections/tests and concrete provisions.

PEC Building Code of Pakistan 2021 ↗

10 / READY-MIX DELIVERY

Receiving concrete at site: checks before discharge

Before unloading a ready-mix truck, compare the delivery ticket with the approved concrete specification and the pour location. Record any discrepancy and ask the responsible engineer/site quality lead to resolve it before discharge.

  • Confirm mix designation/strength, specified slump or workability, aggregate size, admixture and quantity against the approved order.
  • Check ticket details, batch/loading time, truck identification and delivery destination; record arrival and discharge times.
  • Carry out the specified site slump/workability test and sampling/cube or cylinder testing under the project quality plan; record results and sample identification.
  • Do not add water or admixture on site without authorization and documented control under the approved procedure.
  • Check placement access, pump/hoses, vibration equipment, weather and curing arrangements before discharge.
  • Document rejected loads, test results, any authorized adjustment and the responsible approval; do not use concrete that does not meet the specification.

Test method, acceptance range, sampling frequency and delivery limits must come from the approved mix, contract and project quality plan. This checklist is not a substitute for the structural engineer or applicable code.

PEC Building Code of Pakistan 2021 ↗

08 / QUOTATIONS

Compare like-for-like delivered quotes

Before comparing two suppliers, standardise the exact specification and unit: cement by 50 kg bag and type; steel by grade, diameter and tonne/kg; bricks by class and count; sand/crush by material type and measured truck volume. Never compare a Grade 40 PBS bar average with a Grade 60 dealer quote.

For each quote, record the observation date, quantity, delivery point, freight, loading/unloading, taxes, unloading responsibility, breakage allowance and quote validity. A useful comparison is: delivered total = unit rate × quantity + freight + loading/unloading + applicable taxes − documented discounts. Check whether any charge is already included so it is not counted twice.

Use PBS city averages as a dated benchmark for the same item and unit—not as a guaranteed shop price. If the supplier specification differs, request a fresh written quote rather than forcing a comparison. PBS Price Statistics ↗ · Monthly construction items workbook ↗

Frequently asked questions

Common planning questions

Is there one official A+ construction rate per square foot?

No single official fixed A+ rate is stated here. Specification, structural system, soil, covered area, number of floors, finish brands, MEP scope and site access all affect the price. Enter a current written contractor rate in the calculator.

Why do 5 marla or 10 marla plots have different square-foot areas?

Marla conventions vary. This calculator lets you select 225 or 272.25 sq ft per marla, or use exact frontage and depth. The allotment letter and approved site plan should take priority over a generic marla label.

Can I use the CDA covered-area estimate in Bahria or DHA?

No. The CDA Schedule-1 estimate is limited to the selected CDA residential-sector category. Other authorities or schemes may use different rules. Confirm the current building-control authority and approved plan for the exact plot.

Does an approved layout plan mean a housing scheme has an NOC?

Not necessarily. CDA explains that private schemes in ICT have a two-step process: layout-plan approval and, after subsequent formalities, an NOC for development. Check both statuses and any later cancellation or extension notice.

Are the rates on this page guaranteed supplier prices?

No. They are dated published market indicators, not a supplier invoice or SKYLINE quotation. Request a written quote with exact brand, grade, size, quantity, tax and delivery terms before making a purchase.

Practical reference library for owners and site teams

Before construction starts

  • Confirm title, plot boundaries, access and approved land use
  • Obtain current authority requirements and sanctioned drawings
  • Complete soil investigation and structural design where required
  • Prepare BOQ, procurement schedule, cash flow and contractor scope
  • Set site safety, neighbour protection and waste disposal arrangements

During construction

  • Maintain latest drawing set and site instruction register
  • Inspect concealed works before covering and photograph key stages
  • Record concrete tickets, test reports and curing observations; follow the curing guide
  • Check waterproofing before tile/finish installation
  • Reconcile deliveries, measured quantities, variations and payments

Reference sources

  • Pakistan Bureau of Statistics: official price statistics and methodology
  • FBR: tax laws, notices, taxpayer services and valuation references
  • CDA/RDA and relevant development authority: scheme, building and planning records
  • Pakistan Engineering Council: professional and engineering references
  • Manufacturer technical datasheets: installation, storage and warranty conditions

Site handover file

  • As-built drawings and approved revisions
  • Equipment manuals, warranties and supplier contacts
  • Electrical/plumbing test records and commissioning notes
  • Snag list closure and keys/access inventory
  • Final account, maintenance schedule and emergency contacts
