Buyer & seller checklist

Important checks before a property deal.

Before paying a token, advance or deposit, verify the property and all parties independently. The appropriate checks depend on property type and jurisdiction.

  • Confirm seller identity and legal authority to sell.
  • Check title, ownership chain and original documents.
  • Verify society / authority approval, layout and land-use status where applicable.
  • Check possession, encumbrances, litigation, mortgages and outstanding dues.
  • Confirm measurements, boundaries and physical condition.
  • Use a written agreement and obtain professional legal advice before payment or transfer.
SKYLINE's website enquiry is not a title verification, legal opinion, valuation, guarantee of return or confirmation that a property is available. Buyers and sellers should obtain independent legal and official verification.
