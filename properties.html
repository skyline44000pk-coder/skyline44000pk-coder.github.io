Buyer & seller checklist

Important checks before a property deal.

Before paying a token, advance or deposit, verify the property and all parties independently. The appropriate checks depend on property type and jurisdiction.

  • Confirm seller identity and legal authority to sell.
  • Check title, ownership chain and original documents.
  • Verify society / authority approval, layout and land-use status where applicable.
  • Check possession, encumbrances, litigation, mortgages and outstanding dues.
  • Confirm measurements, boundaries and physical condition.
  • Use a written agreement and obtain professional legal advice before payment or transfer.
SKYLINE's website enquiry is not a title verification, legal opinion, valuation, guarantee of return or confirmation that a property is available. Buyers and sellers should obtain independent legal and official verification.

PROPERTY ADVISORY DEPARTMENT

Buyer, seller, landlord & tenant due-diligence guide

Buyer checks

  • Match seller identity with title/ownership documents and authority to sell
  • Verify plot/flat number, dimensions, location and possession on ground
  • Check transfer history, mortgage, litigation, dues and restrictions
  • Confirm scheme approval and the specific block/phase/layout status with regulator
  • Use a written agreement reviewed by a qualified property lawyer

Seller preparation

  • Prepare ownership chain, allotment/transfer letter and possession evidence
  • Collect paid dues, utility status, approved plan and completion records if applicable
  • Disclose encumbrances, tenancy, disputes, defects and outstanding charges
  • Agree commission, token handling, completion conditions and refund terms in writing

Rental checks

  • Inspect condition, fixtures, meter readings and inventory jointly
  • Record rent, deposit, escalation, term, notice and maintenance responsibility
  • Confirm permitted use, occupants, parking, pets and subletting terms
  • Photograph handover condition and sign a dated inventory

Commercial property checks

  • Verify permitted land use, trade licence and building-use restrictions
  • Check access, parking, loading, signage and fire/life-safety requirements
  • Review service charges, common-area rights and utility capacity
  • Check lease term, renewal, fit-out approvals and reinstatement obligations

Transaction safety: Do not rely only on screenshots, photocopies, dealer claims or a society office statement. Independently verify original records with the relevant land/revenue authority, development regulator, registrar or legal professional before paying a token or signing.
