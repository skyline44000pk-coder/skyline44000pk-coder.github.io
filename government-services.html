04 · Before relying on any record

A simple verification process

Use more than one document and confirm the exact property—not only a project name or marketing brochure.

01 · Confirm jurisdiction

Identify city, district, tehsil, mouza/revenue estate, zone and land-owning agency.

02 · Check current approvals

Read the authority's latest LOP, NOC, cancellation, extension and public-notice wording.

03 · Verify the individual plot

Match plot number, block, phase, approved boundary, seller title, possession and encumbrances.

04 · Confirm dues and taxes

Obtain current written challans/clearances from FBR and the correct local/provincial authority.

Important: no automatic legal certification

SKYLINE provides public-information links and educational checklists only. A scheme appearing in an authority list does not prove that every extension, block, file or plot is approved or has clear title. A missing search result does not by itself prove a scheme is illegal. Obtain certified records and written confirmation from the competent authority and independent legal/tax professionals before any payment or transfer.
